# Common Tamil idioms and sayings

## Every place is home — யாதும் ஊரே யாவரும் கேளிர் (yaadhum oore yaavarum kelir)

Literal meaning: "every place is my town, everyone is my kin". This famous Tamil line was written by the poet Kaniyan Poongundranar and comes from the Purananuru, a collection of classical Sangam poetry. It expresses the idea that all people belong to one human family and that no place is foreign. Tamil speakers quote it in speeches and writing about unity, hospitality and welcoming strangers.

## The face shows the heart — அகத்தின் அழகு முகத்தில் தெரியும் (agaththin azhagu mugaththil theriyum)

Literal meaning: "the beauty of the inside (the heart) shows on the face". This Tamil saying means that what a person feels or is like inside — happiness, worry, kindness — can be seen on their face. It is said, for example, when someone looks cheerful because of good news, or when a person's worry is easy to read.

## Know the depth first — ஆழம் தெரியாமல் காலை விடாதே (aazham theriyaamal kaalai vidaadhe)

Literal meaning: "do not put your leg (into the water) without knowing the depth". This Tamil saying is a warning not to get involved in something — a business, a plan, an argument — without first understanding how serious or risky it is. Here காலை (kaalai) is கால் (kaal, leg) with the object ending -ஐ, not the word for "morning".
