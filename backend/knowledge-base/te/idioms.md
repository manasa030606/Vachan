# Common Telugu idioms and sayings

## అందని ద్రాక్ష పుల్లన (andani draaksha pullana)

Literal meaning: "the grapes you cannot reach are sour". Telugu speakers use this saying, like the English "sour grapes", when someone pretends not to want something only because they could not get it. Example: he says he never wanted the job — అందని ద్రాక్ష పుల్లన!

## ఆలస్యం అమృతం విషం (aalasyam amrutam visham)

Literal meaning: "delay turns nectar into poison". This Telugu saying means that a good thing done too late loses its value, so act at the right time. It is often said to someone who keeps postponing a decision.

## కోటి విద్యలు కూటి కొరకే (koti vidyalu kooti korake)

Literal meaning: "a crore (ten million) skills are all for the sake of food". This Telugu saying means that all learning and all kinds of work are, in the end, a way to earn one's living. It is used with a smile when people talk about why they work so hard.
