# Everyday Bengali vocabulary

## Family words

Bengali family words: মা (maa, mother), বাবা (baabaa, father), দাদা (daadaa, elder brother), ভাই (bhaai, brother, often a younger brother), দিদি (didi, elder sister), বোন (bon, sister, often a younger sister), দাদু (daadu, grandfather), ঠাকুমা (thaakumaa, father's mother), দিদিমা (didimaa, mother's mother). A friend is বন্ধু (bondhu), the same word for a man or a woman. Careful: in Bengali দাদা means "elder brother", not "grandfather" as in Hindi.

## Food and drink

<!-- topic: food -->

Bengali food words: খাবার (khaabaar, food), ভাত (bhaat, cooked rice), জল (jol, water), দুধ (dudh, milk), মাছ (maachh, fish), ডাল (daal, lentils), ডিম (dim, egg), নুন (nun, salt), চা (chaa, tea), মিষ্টি (mishti, sweets / sweet). For water, পানি (paani) is used in Bangladesh and by many Muslims, while জল (jol) is common in West Bengal. Bengali often uses খাওয়া (khaaoyaa, to eat) for drinking too: চা খাওয়া (chaa khaaoyaa, to have tea). "I want food" is আমার খাবার চাই (aamaar khaabaar chaai).

## Numbers 1 to 10

<!-- topic: numbers -->

Bengali numbers from one to ten, with the Bengali digits: ১ এক (ek), ২ দুই (dui), ৩ তিন (tin), ৪ চার (chaar), ৫ পাঁচ (paanch), ৬ ছয় (chhoy), ৭ সাত (shaat), ৮ আট (aat), ৯ নয় (noy), ১০ দশ (dosh). Zero is ০ শূন্য (shunno). Before a noun, Bengali usually adds a classifier to the number: একটা বই (ektaa boi, one book), দুটো কলম (duto kolom, two pens).

## Colours

<!-- topic: colours; level: elementary -->

Bengali colour words: লাল (laal, red), সবুজ (shobuj, green), নীল (nil, blue), হলুদ (holud, yellow — also the word for turmeric), সাদা (shaadaa, white), কালো (kaalo, black). The word for colour is রং (rong). Colour words go before the noun and do not change their form: লাল গোলাপ (laal golaap, red rose), সাদা জামা (saadaa jaamaa, white shirt).

## Time words

<!-- topic: time; level: elementary -->

Useful Bengali time words: আজ (aaj, today), কাল (kaal, tomorrow or yesterday — the verb tense tells you which), আগামীকাল (aagaamikaal, tomorrow), গতকাল (gotokaal, yesterday), এখন (ekhon, now), পরে (pore, later / after), সকাল (shokaal, morning), বিকেল (bikel, afternoon), রাত (raat, night). Example: কাল সকালে আসুন (kaal shokaale aashun, "please come tomorrow morning").
