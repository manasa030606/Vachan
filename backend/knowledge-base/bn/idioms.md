# Common Bengali idioms and sayings

## যত গর্জে তত বর্ষে না (joto gorje toto borshe naa)

Literal meaning: "it does not rain as much as it thunders". Bengali speakers use this saying about people who make loud threats or big promises but do little, much like the English "barking dogs seldom bite". Example: he keeps saying he will complain to the manager — যত গর্জে তত বর্ষে না!

## নাচতে না জানলে উঠোন বাঁকা (naachte naa jaanle uthon baankaa)

Literal meaning: "if you can't dance, (you say) the courtyard is crooked". This Bengali saying is used when someone blames other things or other people for their own lack of skill, like the English "a bad workman blames his tools".

## অতি লোভে তাঁতি নষ্ট (oti lobhe taanti noshto)

Literal meaning: "through too much greed the weaver was ruined". This Bengali saying warns that excessive greed leads to loss: by trying to get too much, a person can lose even what they already had. It is said when someone's greed backfires.
