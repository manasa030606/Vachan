# Common Kannada idioms and sayings

## ಹಿತ್ತಲ ಗಿಡ ಮದ್ದಲ್ಲ (hittala gida maddalla)

Literal meaning: "the plant in the backyard is not (thought of as) medicine". This Kannada saying, a ಗಾದೆ (gaade, proverb), means that people often fail to value what is close at hand or familiar, and look for help or talent far away instead. It is said, for example, when a family ignores the advice of their own relative but listens to a stranger saying the same thing.

## ಕೈ ಕೆಸರಾದರೆ ಬಾಯಿ ಮೊಸರು (kai kesaraadare baayi mosaru)

Literal meaning: "if the hand gets muddy, the mouth gets curd". This Kannada gaade comes from farming: only if you work in the muddy field will you have good food to eat. It means that hard work brings its reward, and is often said to encourage someone to put in effort.

## ಆಳಾಗಬಲ್ಲವನು ಅರಸಾಗಬಲ್ಲ (aalaagaballavanu arasaagaballa)

Literal meaning: "one who can be a servant can become a king". This Kannada gaade means that a person who is willing to do humble work and to serve others learns what is needed to lead them well. It is used to praise humility and to remind people that no work is beneath them.
