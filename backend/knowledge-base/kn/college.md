# College and work in Kannada

## Student and teacher — ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ, ಶಿಕ್ಷಕ (vidyaarthi, shikshaka)

<!-- type: vocabulary -->

In Kannada a student is ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ (vidyaarthi); the formal feminine is ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿನಿ (vidyaarthini), though ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ is used for everyone in speech. A teacher is ಶಿಕ್ಷಕ (shikshaka), a female teacher ಶಿಕ್ಷಕಿ (shikshaki); college teachers are often called ಲೆಕ್ಚರರ್ (lekcharar) or ಪ್ರೊಫೆಸರ್ (profesar). Students address teachers as ಸಾರ್ (saar) or ಮೇಡಂ (medam) and always use ನೀವು (neevu) with them. Examples: ನಾನು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ (naanu vidyaarthi, I am a student); ಅವರು ನಮ್ಮ ಟೀಚರ್ (avaru namma teechar, she/he is our teacher).

Also asked as: how to say student in Kannada, teacher in Kannada, how to address a teacher, I am a student

## What are you studying? — ನೀವು ಏನು ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu enu oduttiddeeri?)

In Kannada, "to study" in everyday speech is ಓದು (odu, literally "read"). To ask what someone studies: ನೀವು ಏನು ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu enu oduttiddeeri?), spoken ಏನ್ ಓದ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಾ? (en odtiddeeraa?); to a classmate: ಏನ್ ಓದ್ತಿದ್ದೀಯಾ? (en odtiddeeyaa?). Answer: ನಾನು ಇಂಜಿನಿಯರಿಂಗ್ ಓದುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ (naanu injiniyaring oduttiddene, I'm studying engineering), spoken ಓದ್ತಿದ್ದೀನಿ (odtiddeeni). For the year: ನಾನು ಮೂರನೇ ವರ್ಷದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೇನೆ (naanu moorane varshadalli iddene, I'm in third year). Course names (BTech, BCom, MBA) are said in English.

Also asked as: what are you studying in Kannada, which course, which year are you in, I study engineering

## Class — ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ (klaas)

Kannada students say ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ (klaas) for a class or classroom; the formal word is ತರಗತಿ (taragati). Useful phrases: ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (klaas ellide?, where is the class?), ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ? (klaas eshtu gantege?, what time is the class?), ಇವತ್ತು ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಇದೆಯಾ? (ivattu klaas ideyaa?, is there class today?), ಇವತ್ತು ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಇಲ್ಲ (ivattu klaas illa, no class today). "The class has started" is ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಶುರು ಆಯ್ತು (klaas shuru aaytu), and "the class is over" ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಮುಗೀತು (klaas mugeetu), written ಮುಗಿಯಿತು (mugiyitu).

Also asked as: class in Kannada, is there class today, where is the classroom, class is over, lecture

## Late for class — ಒಳಗೆ ಬರಬಹುದಾ? (olage barabahudaa?)

<!-- topic: politeness -->

If you are late for a class in Kannada, knock and ask ಒಳಗೆ ಬರಬಹುದಾ, ಸಾರ್? (olage barabahudaa, saar?, may I come in, sir?), or ಮೇಡಂ (medam) for a woman; the written form is ಬರಬಹುದೇ? (barabahude?). Apologise: ಸಾರಿ ಸಾರ್, ಲೇಟ್ ಆಯ್ತು (saari saar, let aaytu, sorry sir, I'm late). Give a reason if asked: ಬಸ್ ಲೇಟ್ ಆಯ್ತು (bas let aaytu, the bus was late), ಟ್ರಾಫಿಕ್ ಇತ್ತು (traafik ittu, there was traffic). The teacher may say ಬನ್ನಿ (banni, come in) or ಕೂತ್ಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (kootkolli, sit down).

Also asked as: may I come in in Kannada, sorry I'm late, late for class, asking permission to enter

## Exam — ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ (pareekshe)

<!-- type: vocabulary -->

In Kannada an exam is ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ (pareekshe); students also say ಎಕ್ಸಾಮ್ (eksaam). Useful phrases: ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಯಾವಾಗ? (pareekshe yaavaaga?, when is the exam?), ನನಗೆ ನಾಳೆ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಇದೆ (nanage naale pareekshe ide, I have an exam tomorrow), ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಹೇಗಾಯ್ತು? (pareekshe hegaaytu?, how did the exam go?). Answers: ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿ ಆಯ್ತು (chennaagi aaytu, it went well) or ಪರವಾಗಿಲ್ಲ (paravaagilla, it was okay). Results are ಫಲಿತಾಂಶ (phalitaamsha) or ರಿಸಲ್ಟ್ (risalt). To wish someone luck: ಆಲ್ ದ ಬೆಸ್ಟ್ (aal da best, all the best) is what people actually say.

Also asked as: exam in Kannada, how did the exam go, I have an exam tomorrow, results, good luck

## Homework and assignments — ಹೋಂವರ್ಕ್ (homvark)

<!-- type: vocabulary -->

In Kannada, homework is formally ಮನೆಗೆಲಸ (manegelasa), literally "house-work" (it also means household chores), but students usually say ಹೋಂವರ್ಕ್ (homvark). College work is ಅಸೈನ್‌ಮೆಂಟ್ (asainment) or ಪ್ರಾಜೆಕ್ಟ್ (praajekt). Useful phrases: ಹೋಂವರ್ಕ್ ಮುಗೀತಾ? (homvark mugeetaa?, did you finish the homework?), ಇನ್ನೂ ಮುಗಿದಿಲ್ಲ (innoo mugidilla, not finished yet), ಅಸೈನ್‌ಮೆಂಟ್ ಯಾವಾಗ ಕೊಡಬೇಕು? (asainment yaavaaga kodabeku?, when do we have to submit the assignment?). "Notes" are ನೋಟ್ಸ್ (nots): ನೋಟ್ಸ್ ಕೊಡ್ತೀಯಾ? (nots kodteeyaa?, will you give me your notes?).

Also asked as: homework in Kannada, assignment, did you finish, when is the submission, can I borrow your notes

## I have a doubt — ಒಂದು ಡೌಟ್ (ondu daut)

<!-- topic: college -->

In Indian colleges, Kannada-speaking students say ಸಾರ್, ಒಂದು ಡೌಟ್ (saar, ondu daut) to mean "sir, I have a question" — "doubt" in Indian English means a question about something unclear. The formal Kannada word is ಸಂದೇಹ (sandeha), and "question" is ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ (prashne): ಒಂದು ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಕೇಳಬಹುದಾ? (ondu prashne kelabahudaa?, may I ask a question?). To ask for an explanation: ಇದನ್ನು ಇನ್ನೊಮ್ಮೆ ವಿವರಿಸುತ್ತೀರಾ? (idannu innomme vivarisutteeraa?, could you explain this again?). "I understood" is ಅರ್ಥ ಆಯ್ತು (artha aaytu).

Also asked as: how to ask a question in class in Kannada, I have a doubt, may I ask a question, explain again

## College places — ಲೈಬ್ರರಿ, ಕ್ಯಾಂಟೀನ್ (laibrari, kyaanteen)

<!-- type: vocabulary -->

Most Kannada-speaking students use English words for college places: ಲೈಬ್ರರಿ (laibrari, library — formal ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯ, granthaalaya), ಕ್ಯಾಂಟೀನ್ (kyaanteen, canteen), ಲ್ಯಾಬ್ (lyaab, lab), ಹಾಸ್ಟೆಲ್ (haastel, hostel), ಆಫೀಸ್ (aafees, office), ಪ್ರಿನ್ಸಿಪಾಲ್ (prinsipaal, principal). Ask with ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ellide?): ಲೈಬ್ರರಿ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (laibrari ellide?, where is the library?). Invite a friend: ಕ್ಯಾಂಟೀನ್‌ಗೆ ಹೋಗೋಣ (kyaanteenge hogona, let's go to the canteen). "Ground / playground" is ಮೈದಾನ (maidaana) or ಗ್ರೌಂಡ್ (graund).

Also asked as: library in Kannada, canteen, hostel, places in college, where is the library

## What do you do? (job) — ನೀವು ಏನು ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀರಿ? (neevu enu kelasa maadutteeri?)

<!-- topic: work -->

To ask someone's job in Kannada, say ನೀವು ಏನು ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೀರಿ? (neevu enu kelasa maadutteeri?, what work do you do?), spoken ಏನ್ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡ್ತೀರಾ? (en kelasa maadteeraa?); ಕೆಲಸ (kelasa) means work or job. Answers: ನಾನು ಆಫೀಸಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆ (naanu aafeesinalli kelasa maaduttene, I work in an office); ನಾನು ಸಾಫ್ಟ್‌ವೇರ್ ಇಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ (naanu saaftver injiniyar, I'm a software engineer); ನಾನು ಟೀಚರ್ (naanu teechar, I'm a teacher). "Where do you work?" is ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡ್ತೀರಾ? (elli kelasa maadteeraa?). The formal word for employment is ಉದ್ಯೋಗ (udyoga).

Also asked as: what is your job in Kannada, what do you do for work, where do you work, I am an engineer

## Office and colleagues — ಆಫೀಸ್, ಕೊಲೀಗ್ (aafees, koleeg)

<!-- type: vocabulary; topic: work -->

In Kannada an office is ಆಫೀಸ್ (aafees); government offices are ಕಚೇರಿ (kacheri). A colleague is formally ಸಹೋದ್ಯೋಗಿ (sahodyogi), but people mostly say ಕೊಲೀಗ್ (koleeg) or "office friend". Your boss is the ಮ್ಯಾನೇಜರ್ (myaanejar) or ಬಾಸ್ (baas); the formal word is ಮೇಲಧಿಕಾರಿ (meladhikaari). Examples: ಇವರು ನನ್ನ ಕೊಲೀಗ್ (ivaru nanna koleeg, this is my colleague); ನಾನು ಒಂಬತ್ತು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಆಫೀಸಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆ (naanu ombattu gantege aafeesige hoguttene, I go to the office at nine). Use ನೀವು (neevu) and ಅವರು (avaru) for colleagues unless you are close friends.

Also asked as: office in Kannada, colleague in Kannada, boss, manager, coworker

## Meeting — ಮೀಟಿಂಗ್ (meeting)

<!-- topic: work -->

In Kannada workplaces, "meeting" is said in English: ಮೀಟಿಂಗ್ (meeting); the formal Kannada word is ಸಭೆ (sabhe), used for public or official meetings. Useful phrases: ನಾನು ಮೀಟಿಂಗ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ (naanu meetingnalli iddeeni, I'm in a meeting), ಆಮೇಲೆ ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡ್ತೀನಿ (aamele fon maadteeni, I'll call later), ಮೀಟಿಂಗ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ? (meeting eshtu gantege?, what time is the meeting?), ಮೀಟಿಂಗ್ ಮುಗೀತು (meeting mugeetu, the meeting is over). To excuse yourself: ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಬ್ಯುಸಿ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ (svalpa byusi iddeeni, I'm a bit busy).

Also asked as: meeting in Kannada, I'm in a meeting, I'm busy, what time is the meeting, call you later

## Taking leave — ರಜೆ (raje)

<!-- topic: work -->

In Kannada, a holiday or leave from work or school is ರಜೆ (raje), also ರಜಾ (rajaa); people also say ಲೀವ್ (leev). Useful phrases: ನಾಳೆ ನಾನು ರಜೆ ತಗೊಳ್ತೀನಿ (naale naanu raje tagolteeni, I'm taking leave tomorrow), ಇವತ್ತು ರಜೆ (ivattu raje, today is a holiday), ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ದಿನ ರಜೆ ಬೇಕು (nanage ondu dina raje beku, I need a day off). Asking a manager politely: ನಾಳೆ ರಜೆ ತಗೋಬಹುದಾ? (naale raje tagobahudaa?, may I take leave tomorrow?). Summer holidays are ಬೇಸಿಗೆ ರಜೆ (besige raje).

Also asked as: leave in Kannada, holiday, I need a day off, can I take leave tomorrow, vacation

## Work is over — ಕೆಲಸ ಮುಗೀತು (kelasa mugeetu)

<!-- topic: work -->

To say work is finished in Kannada, say ಕೆಲಸ ಮುಗೀತು (kelasa mugeetu), written ಕೆಲಸ ಮುಗಿಯಿತು (kelasa mugiyitu); ಮುಗಿ (mugi) means to finish. "Not finished yet" is ಇನ್ನೂ ಮುಗಿದಿಲ್ಲ (innoo mugidilla). Friends ask ಕೆಲಸ ಆಯ್ತಾ? (kelasa aaytaa?, done with work?) or ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ ಫ್ರೀ ಆಗ್ತೀಯಾ? (eshtu gantege free aagteeyaa?, what time will you be free?). Leaving: ನಾನು ಹೊರಡ್ತೀನಿ (naanu horadteeni, I'm leaving now), written ಹೊರಡುತ್ತೇನೆ (horaduttene). Colleagues reply ಸರಿ, ನಾಳೆ ಸಿಗೋಣ (sari, naale sigona, okay, see you tomorrow).

Also asked as: I finished work in Kannada, are you done, I'm leaving, what time will you be free, work is over

## Salary and interviews — ಸಂಬಳ, ಇಂಟರ್‌ವ್ಯೂ (sambala, intarvyoo)

<!-- type: vocabulary; topic: work; level: intermediate -->

Kannada work vocabulary: ಸಂಬಳ (sambala, salary; people also say ಸ್ಯಾಲರಿ, syaalari), ಇಂಟರ್‌ವ್ಯೂ (intarvyoo, interview), ಕಂಪನಿ (kampani, company), ಹೊಸ ಕೆಲಸ (hosa kelasa, new job), ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಕೆಲಸ (sarkaari kelasa, government job). Examples: ನನಗೆ ನಾಳೆ ಇಂಟರ್‌ವ್ಯೂ ಇದೆ (nanage naale intarvyoo ide, I have an interview tomorrow); ನನಗೆ ಹೊಸ ಕೆಲಸ ಸಿಕ್ಕಿತು (nanage hosa kelasa sikkitu, I got a new job), spoken ಸಿಕ್ತು (siktu). Asking someone's salary directly is considered personal. Congratulate with ಅಭಿನಂದನೆಗಳು (abhinandanegalu).

Also asked as: salary in Kannada, job interview, I got a new job, company, government job
