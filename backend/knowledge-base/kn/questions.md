# Asking questions in Kannada

## What? — ಏನು? (enu?)

<!-- type: phrase -->

"What" in Kannada is ಏನು (enu), often shortened in speech to ಏನ್ (en). It usually comes just before the verb, or at the end when there is no verb: ಇದು ಏನು? (idu enu?) = what is this? ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (nimma hesaru enu?) = what is your name? ನೀವು ಏನು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu enu maaduttiddeeri?) = what are you doing? ಏನು joins with following words in speech: ಏನಾಯ್ತು? (enaaytu?, what happened?), written ಏನಾಯಿತು? (enaayitu?). On its own, ಏನು? also works as "sorry, what?" when you did not hear; the more polite form is ಏನಂದ್ರಿ? (enandri?, what did you say?).

## Who? — ಯಾರು? (yaaru?)

<!-- type: phrase -->

"Who" in Kannada is ಯಾರು (yaaru): ಅವರು ಯಾರು? (avaru yaaru?) = who is he/she? ಇವರು ಯಾರು? (ivaru yaaru?) = who is this (person)? ಯಾರು ಬಂದರು? (yaaru bandaru?) = who came? On the phone: ಯಾರು ಮಾತಾಡ್ತಿರೋದು? (yaaru maataadtirodu?, who is speaking?). "Whose" is ಯಾರ (yaara): ಇದು ಯಾರ ಪುಸ್ತಕ? (idu yaara pustaka?) = whose book is this? "To whom / for whom" is ಯಾರಿಗೆ (yaarige): ಇದು ಯಾರಿಗೆ? (who is this for?). With -ಊ it becomes "nobody": ಯಾರೂ ಇಲ್ಲ (yaaroo illa, there is nobody).

Also asked as: how to say who in Kannada, who is this, whose is this, who is speaking, Kannada word for who

## Where? — ಎಲ್ಲಿ? (elli?)

<!-- type: phrase; topic: directions -->

"Where" in Kannada is ಎಲ್ಲಿ (elli). "Where is it?" joins into one word: ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ellide?) — put the place first: ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (bas nildaana ellide?, where is the bus stand?). For people: ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu elliddeeri?, where are you?). Endings change the meaning: ಎಲ್ಲಿಗೆ (ellige, to where): ಎಲ್ಲಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (ellige hoguttiddeeri?, where are you going?); ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ (ellinda, from where): ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಬಂದಿರಿ? (ellinda bandiri?, where did you come from?). "Where are you from?" is ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿಯವರು? (neevu elliyavaru?).

Also asked as: how to say where in Kannada, where is it, where are you going, where are you from, Kannada word for where

## When? — ಯಾವಾಗ? (yaavaaga?)

<!-- type: phrase; topic: time -->

"When" in Kannada is ಯಾವಾಗ (yaavaaga): ಬಸ್ ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ? (bas yaavaaga baruttade?) = when does the bus come?, spoken ಬಸ್ ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತೆ? (barutte?). ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಯಾವಾಗ? (pareekshe yaavaaga?) = when is the exam? ನೀವು ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತೀರಿ? (neevu yaavaaga barutteeri?) = when will you come? For a clock time, ask ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ? (eshtu gantege?, at what time?): ಕ್ಲಾಸ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆಗೆ? (what time is the class?). With -ಊ it means "always": ಯಾವಾಗಲೂ (yaavaagaloo).

Also asked as: how to say when in Kannada, when is the bus, at what time, when will you come, Kannada word for when

## Why? — ಯಾಕೆ? (yaake?)

<!-- type: phrase -->

"Why" in Kannada is ಯಾಕೆ (yaake); the formal written word is ಏಕೆ (eke). ಯಾಕೆ ತಡವಾಯಿತು? (yaake tadavaayitu?) = why are you late? (spoken ಯಾಕೆ ಲೇಟ್ ಆಯ್ತು?). ನೀವು ಯಾಕೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu yaake kannada kaliyuttiddeeri?) = why are you learning Kannada? To answer "because", Kannada usually gives the reason followed by ಅದಕ್ಕೆ (adakke, that's why), or starts with ಯಾಕೆಂದರೆ (yaakendare, because), spoken ಯಾಕಂದ್ರೆ (yaakandre). Example: ಟ್ರಾಫಿಕ್ ಇತ್ತು, ಅದಕ್ಕೆ ಲೇಟ್ ಆಯ್ತು (traafik ittu, adakke let aaytu, there was traffic, that's why I'm late). A plain ಯಾಕೆ? can sound sharp, so say it gently.

Also asked as: how to say why in Kannada, because in Kannada, why are you late, asking the reason

## How? — ಹೇಗೆ? (hege?)

<!-- type: phrase -->

"How" in Kannada is ಹೇಗೆ (hege): ಸ್ಟೇಷನ್‌ಗೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೇಕು? (steshange hege hogabeku?) = how do I get to the station? ಇದನ್ನು ಹೇಗೆ ಮಾಡುವುದು? (idannu hege maaduvudu?) = how do you do this?, spoken ಹೇಗೆ ಮಾಡೋದು? (hege maadodu?). ಹೇಗೆ joins with ಇದೆ (ide, is) to ask "how is it?": ಊಟ ಹೇಗಿದೆ? (oota hegide?) = how is the food? With people it asks "how are you": ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu hegiddeeri?). To ask how something went: ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಹೇಗಾಯ್ತು? (pareekshe hegaaytu?, how did the exam go?).

Also asked as: how to say how in Kannada, how do I get there, how is it, how did it go, Kannada word for how

## How much? How many? — ಎಷ್ಟು? (eshtu?)

<!-- type: phrase; topic: numbers -->

Kannada uses one word, ಎಷ್ಟು (eshtu), for both "how much" and "how many". Price: ಇದು ಎಷ್ಟು? (idu eshtu?) or ಇದರ ಬೆಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (idara bele eshtu?) = how much is this? Quantity: ಎಷ್ಟು ಬೇಕು? (eshtu beku?) = how much / how many do you want? Time: ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆ ಆಯಿತು? (eshtu gante aayitu?) = what time is it? Distance: ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ? (eshtu doora?, how far?). For people, add ಜನ (jana): ಎಷ್ಟು ಜನ ಇದ್ದಾರೆ? (eshtu jana iddaare?, how many people are there?). For ordinal "which number": ಎಷ್ಟನೇ (eshtane): ಇವತ್ತು ಎಷ್ಟನೇ ತಾರೀಖು? (what's today's date?).

Also asked as: how to say how much in Kannada, how many, how many people, eshtu meaning, asking quantity

## Which? — ಯಾವ / ಯಾವುದು (yaava / yaavudu)

<!-- type: phrase -->

Kannada has two forms of "which". Before a noun use ಯಾವ (yaava): ಯಾವ ಬಸ್? (yaava bas?, which bus?), ಯಾವ ಬಣ್ಣ? (yaava banna?, which colour?), ಇವತ್ತು ಯಾವ ವಾರ? (ivattu yaava vaara?, what day is it today?). Standing alone, "which one" is ಯಾವುದು (yaavudu): ನಿಮಗೆ ಯಾವುದು ಬೇಕು? (nimage yaavudu beku?) = which one do you want? ನಿಮ್ಮ ಊರು ಯಾವುದು? (nimma ooru yaavudu?) = which is your home town? For people, "which person" is ಯಾರು (yaaru, who). The answer often uses ಇದು (idu, this one) or ಅದು (adu, that one).

## Yes/no questions — add -ಆ (-aa)

<!-- topic: yes-no-questions -->

To turn a Kannada statement into a yes/no question, add -ಆ (-aa) to the last word, usually the verb. ನೀವು ಬರುತ್ತೀರಿ (neevu barutteeri, you will come) → ನೀವು ಬರುತ್ತೀರಾ? (neevu barutteeraa?, will you come?). ಇದೆ (ide, there is) → ಇದೆಯಾ? (ideyaa?, is there?). ಇದು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪುಸ್ತಕ (this is your book) → ಇದು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪುಸ್ತಕವಾ? (pustakavaa?, is this your book?). The word order does not change, and there is no extra word like "do". Formal written Kannada uses -ಏ (-e) instead: ಇದೆಯೇ? (ideye?), ಬರುತ್ತೀರೇ? Rising intonation alone can also make a question in casual speech: ಊಟ ಆಯ್ತು? (oota aaytu?).

## Where does the question word go?

<!-- topic: word-order -->

In Kannada, the question word stays where the answer would be — usually just before the verb — instead of moving to the front as in English. Compare: ನೀವು ನಾಳೆ ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೀರಿ (you will go to Mysuru tomorrow) and ನೀವು ನಾಳೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತೀರಿ? (neevu naale ellige hogutteeri?, where will you go tomorrow?). ನೀವು ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತೀರಿ? (neevu yaavaaga barutteeri?) = when will you come? In "X is what?" questions with no verb, it goes at the end: ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (nimma hesaru enu?). The verb stays last, and no helper word like "do" or "does" is needed.

## Answering yes and no

<!-- type: phrase; topic: yes-no-questions -->

Kannada answers to yes/no questions usually repeat the verb instead of just saying "yes". ಬರುತ್ತೀರಾ? (barutteeraa?, will you come?) → ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ (baruttene, I will come), spoken ಬರ್ತೀನಿ (barteeni); ಇದೆಯಾ? (is there?) → ಇದೆ (ide, there is) or ಇಲ್ಲ (illa, there isn't). ಹೌದು (haudu) means "yes, that's right", for confirming facts: ನೀವು ಆಶಾ ಅಲ್ವಾ? — ಹೌದು (you're Asha, right? — yes). ಇಲ್ಲ (illa) is the general "no"; ಅಲ್ಲ (alla) means "no, it isn't that". To agree to a request say ಸರಿ (sari) or ಆಯ್ತು (aaytu, okay). A casual "yes" is ಹೂಂ (hoom).

## Isn't it? Right? — ಅಲ್ವಾ? (alvaa?)

<!-- type: phrase; topic: everyday-conversation -->

To add "right?" or "isn't it?" to a Kannada sentence, put ಅಲ್ವಾ? (alvaa?) at the end; the written form is ಅಲ್ಲವೇ? (allave?). ಇವತ್ತು ಸೋಮವಾರ, ಅಲ್ವಾ? (ivattu somavaara, alvaa?) = today is Monday, right? ನೀವು ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನವರು, ಅಲ್ವಾ? (neevu bengaloorinavaru, alvaa?) = you are from Bengaluru, aren't you? To react to news with "really?", say ಹೌದಾ? (haudaa?), very common in conversation. ನಿಜವಾಗಲೂ? (nijavaagaloo?) means "truly? seriously?".

## What is this called in Kannada?

<!-- type: phrase; topic: learning-kannada -->

When learning Kannada, the most useful question is ಇದನ್ನ ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಏನಂತಾರೆ? (idanna kannadadalli enantaare?) = what do they call this in Kannada? The more careful form is ಇದನ್ನು ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೇಳುತ್ತಾರೆ? (idannu kannadadalli hege heluttaare?, how do you say this in Kannada?). For a word you heard: … ಅಂದರೆ ಏನು? (… andare enu?, what does … mean?), for example ಸಾಕು ಅಂದರೆ ಏನು? (what does saaku mean?). ಇದರ ಅರ್ಥ ಏನು? (idara artha enu?) = what does this mean? Most Kannada speakers are delighted to help a learner.

Also asked as: how do you say this in Kannada, what does this word mean, what is this called, asking for a word

## Is there …? Do you have …? — … ಇದೆಯಾ? (… ideyaa?)

<!-- type: phrase; topic: shopping -->

To ask "is there …?" or "do you have …?" in Kannada (in a shop, hotel or home), name the thing and add ಇದೆಯಾ? (ideyaa?): ಮಾವಿನಹಣ್ಣು ಇದೆಯಾ? (maavinahannu ideyaa?) = do you have mangoes? ಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ ಇದೆಯಾ? (chillare ideyaa?) = do you have change? ರೂಮ್ ಖಾಲಿ ಇದೆಯಾ? (room khaali ideyaa?) = is a room free? The answer is ಇದೆ (ide, there is / yes) or ಇಲ್ಲ (illa, there isn't / no). To ask what is available: ಏನೇನು ಇದೆ? (enenu ide?, what all is there?). The written form is ಇದೆಯೇ? (ideye?).

Also asked as: how to say do you have in Kannada, is there any, asking if something is available, ideyaa meaning
