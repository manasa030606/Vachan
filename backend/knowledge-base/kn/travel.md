# Travel and directions in Kannada

## Buying a ticket — ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (ondu tiket kodi)

To buy a ticket in Kannada, say the place with -ಗೆ (-ge, to), the number, and ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (tiket kodi, please give a ticket): ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ ಒಂದು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (maisoorige ondu tiket kodi) = one ticket to Mysuru, please. Two tickets: ಎರಡು ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಕೊಡಿ (eradu tiket kodi). On a city bus the conductor (ಕಂಡಕ್ಟರ್, kandaktar) comes to you; just say where you are going: ಮೆಜೆಸ್ಟಿಕ್, ಒಂದು (mejestik, ondu, Majestic, one). Ask the fare: ಟಿಕೆಟ್ ಎಷ್ಟು? (tiket eshtu?, how much is the ticket?). The conductor may ask ಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ ಇದೆಯಾ? (chillare ideyaa?, do you have change?).

Also asked as: how to buy a ticket in Kannada, one ticket please, how much is the ticket, bus ticket, train ticket

## Taking the bus — ಈ ಬಸ್ … ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾ? (ee bas … hoguttaa?)

To check a bus route in Kannada, ask ಈ ಬಸ್ ಮೆಜೆಸ್ಟಿಕ್‌ಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾ? (ee bas mejestikge hoguttaa?, does this bus go to Majestic?); the written form is ಹೋಗುತ್ತದೆಯೇ? (hoguttadeye?). To ask when it comes: ಬಸ್ ಯಾವಾಗ ಬರುತ್ತೆ? (bas yaavaaga barutte?). To ask which bus: ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ ಯಾವ ಬಸ್? (maisoorige yaava bas?, which bus to Mysuru?). Getting on is ಹತ್ತು (hattu) and getting off is ಇಳಿ (ili): ನಾನು ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇಳಿಯಬೇಕು (naanu illi iliyabeku, I need to get off here). City buses in Bengaluru are BMTC; intercity buses are KSRTC.

Also asked as: how to ask about buses in Kannada, does this bus go to, which bus, when does the bus come, getting off the bus

## Taking the train or metro — ರೈಲು, ಮೆಟ್ರೋ (railu, metro)

The Kannada word for train is ರೈಲು (railu); people also say ಟ್ರೈನ್ (train). The station is ರೈಲು ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (railu nildaana), usually called ಸ್ಟೇಷನ್ (steshan). Useful questions: ಮೈಸೂರು ರೈಲು ಯಾವ ಪ್ಲಾಟ್ಫಾರ್ಮ್‌ಗೆ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ? (maisooru railu yaava plaatfaarmge baruttade?, which platform does the Mysuru train come to?) and ರೈಲು ತಡವಾಗಿದೆಯಾ? (railu tadavaagideyaa?, is the train late?). Bengaluru's metro is ನಮ್ಮ ಮೆಟ್ರೋ (namma metro, "our metro"). "Next station" is ಮುಂದಿನ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ (mundina nildaana), which you will hear in metro announcements.

Also asked as: train in Kannada, how to take the metro, which platform, railway station, next station

## Taking an auto — … ಬರ್ತೀರಾ? (… barteeraa?)

<!-- topic: transport -->

To take an auto-rickshaw (ಆಟೋ, aato) in Bengaluru, name the place with -ಗೆ (to) and ask ಬರ್ತೀರಾ? (barteeraa?, will you come?): ಜಯನಗರಕ್ಕೆ ಬರ್ತೀರಾ? (jayanagarakke barteeraa?, will you go to Jayanagar?); the written form is ಬರುತ್ತೀರಾ? (barutteeraa?). Then: ಮೀಟರ್ ಹಾಕಿ (meetar haaki, please put the meter on). If the driver quotes a price, ask ಮೀಟರ್ ಮೇಲೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (meetar mele eshtu?, how much over the meter?) or bargain with ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ (svalpa kadime maadi). Addressing the driver as ಅಣ್ಣ (anna, elder brother) or ಸಾರ್ (saar) sounds friendly. Many people now book autos and cabs through apps.

Also asked as: how to talk to an auto driver in Kannada, auto rickshaw, put the meter, will you come to, how much by auto

## Taxi and cab — ಟ್ಯಾಕ್ಸಿ, ಕ್ಯಾಬ್ (tyaaksi, kyaab)

In Kannada a taxi is ಟ್ಯಾಕ್ಸಿ (tyaaksi), and an app taxi is a ಕ್ಯಾಬ್ (kyaab). Useful phrases with the driver: ಏರ್ಪೋರ್ಟ್‌ಗೆ ಎಷ್ಟು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (erportge eshtu aagutte?, how much to the airport?), ಎಷ್ಟು ಹೊತ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (eshtu hottu aagutte?, how long will it take?), ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಬೇಗ ಹೋಗಿ (svalpa bega hogi, please go a bit faster), ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (svalpa nidhaanavaagi hogi, please go slowly). App drivers often phone and ask ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿದ್ದೀರಿ? (neevu elliddeeri?, where are you?); answer with a landmark: ನಾನು ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ಎದುರು ಇದ್ದೀನಿ (naanu byaank eduru iddeeni, I'm opposite the bank).

Also asked as: how to talk to a taxi driver in Kannada, cab, how much to the airport, where are you, go faster

## How far is it? — ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ ಇದೆ? (eshtu doora ide?)

<!-- topic: directions -->

To ask "how far is it?" in Kannada, say ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ ಇದೆ? (eshtu doora ide?); ದೂರ (doora) means far / distance. With a place: ಸ್ಟೇಷನ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ ಇದೆ? (steshan eshtu doora ide?, how far is the station?). Possible answers: ಹತ್ತಿರ ಇದೆ (hattira ide, it's near), ಇಲ್ಲೇ ಹತ್ತಿರ (ille hattira, right nearby), ದೂರ ಇದೆ (doora ide, it's far), ಜಾಸ್ತಿ ದೂರ ಇಲ್ಲ (jaasti doora illa, not too far), ಸುಮಾರು ಎರಡು ಕಿಲೋಮೀಟರ್ (sumaaru eradu kilomeetar, about two kilometres). "Can I walk there?" is ನಡೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಹೋಗಬಹುದಾ? (nadedukondu hogabahudaa?).

Also asked as: how to ask how far in Kannada, is it near, is it far, distance in Kannada, can I walk there

## How long does it take? — ಎಷ್ಟು ಹೊತ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (eshtu hottu aagutte?)

To ask how long a trip takes in Kannada, say ಎಷ್ಟು ಹೊತ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (eshtu hottu aagutte?); the written form is ಎಷ್ಟು ಹೊತ್ತು ಆಗುತ್ತದೆ? (aaguttade?). ಹೊತ್ತು (hottu) means a stretch of time; people also say ಎಷ್ಟು ಟೈಮ್ ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (eshtu taim aagutte?). For longer trips: ಮೈಸೂರಿಗೆ ಎಷ್ಟು ಗಂಟೆ ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? (maisoorige eshtu gante aagutte?, how many hours to Mysuru?). Answers: ಅರ್ಧ ಗಂಟೆ (ardha gante, half an hour), ಸುಮಾರು ಹತ್ತು ನಿಮಿಷ (sumaaru hattu nimisha, about ten minutes), ಮೂರು ಗಂಟೆ (mooru gante, three hours). In Bengaluru, everyone adds: ಟ್ರಾಫಿಕ್ ಇದ್ದರೆ ಜಾಸ್ತಿ (traafik iddare jaasti, more if there's traffic).

Also asked as: how long does it take in Kannada, how many hours, travel time, how many minutes

## Stop here — ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (illi nillisi)

To ask a driver to stop in Kannada, say ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (illi nillisi, please stop here); in speech ಇಲ್ಲೇ ನಿಲ್ಸಿ (ille nilsi, stop right here). ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸು (nillisu) means to stop something (a vehicle); ನಿಲ್ಲು (nillu) means to stand or stop yourself. Other useful phrases: ಮುಂದೆ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (munde nillisi, stop a bit ahead), ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಹತ್ತಿರ ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ (signal hattira nillisi, stop near the signal), ನಾನು ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಇಳಿಯಬೇಕು (naanu illi iliyabeku, I need to get off here). On a bus you can tell the conductor ಮುಂದಿನ ಸ್ಟಾಪ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಇಳಿಯಬೇಕು (mundina staapnalli iliyabeku, I need to get off at the next stop).

Also asked as: how to say stop here in Kannada, stop the car, I want to get off, next stop, pull over here

## Left, right, straight — ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ, ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ, ನೇರವಾಗಿ

<!-- topic: directions -->

Kannada direction words: ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ (edakke, to the left), ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ (balakke, to the right), ನೇರವಾಗಿ (neravaagi, straight ahead), in speech also ಸೀದಾ (seedaa). With ತಿರುಗಿ (tirugi, please turn): ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ (edakke tirugi, turn left), ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ (balakke tirugi, turn right); with ಹೋಗಿ (hogi, please go): ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ (neravaagi hogi, go straight). "On the left side" is ಎಡಗಡೆ (edagade) and "on the right side" ಬಲಗಡೆ (balagade): ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್ ಬಲಗಡೆ ಇದೆ (byaank balagade ide, the bank is on the right). "Then" is ಆಮೇಲೆ (aamele): ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ, ಆಮೇಲೆ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ.

Also asked as: left and right in Kannada, turn left, go straight, giving directions, on the right side

## Asking the way — … ಗೆ ದಾರಿ ಯಾವುದು? (… ge daari yaavudu?)

<!-- topic: directions -->

To ask the way in Kannada, start with ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನೋಡಿ (svalpa nodi, excuse me), then either name the place with ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ellide?, where is it?) — ಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (aaspatre ellide?, where is the hospital?) — or ask for the route: ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ ದಾರಿ ಯಾವುದು? (bas nildaanakke daari yaavudu?, which is the way to the bus stand?); ದಾರಿ (daari) means way. Directions in Karnataka use landmarks: ಹತ್ತಿರ (hattira, near), ಎದುರು (eduru, opposite), ಪಕ್ಕ (pakka, next to), ಹಿಂದೆ (hinde, behind), ಸರ್ಕಲ್ (sarkal, roundabout). Example answer: ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಎದುರು ಇದೆ (devasthaanada eduru ide, it's opposite the temple).

Also asked as: how to ask for directions in Kannada, which way to, how do I get to, where is the hospital, landmarks

## I am lost — ನಾನು ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿದ್ದೇನೆ (naanu daari tappiddene)

To say "I am lost" in Kannada, say ನಾನು ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿದ್ದೇನೆ (naanu daari tappiddene), literally "I have missed the way"; spoken ದಾರಿ ತಪ್ಪಿದ್ದೀನಿ (daari tappiddeeni). Also natural: ನನಗೆ ದಾರಿ ಗೊತ್ತಾಗುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ (nanage daari gottaaguttilla, I can't find the way). Show an address and ask ಈ ವಿಳಾಸ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? (ee vilaasa ellide?, where is this address?). ಈ ಜಾಗದ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು? (ee jaagada hesaru enu?) means "what is this place called?". Shopkeepers and auto drivers are usually the best people to ask.

Also asked as: how to say I am lost in Kannada, I can't find the way, where is this address, what is this place

## Finding a place to stay — ರೂಮ್ ಸಿಗುತ್ತಾ? (room siguttaa?)

<!-- topic: hotel -->

In Karnataka, ಹೋಟೆಲ್ (hotel) usually means a restaurant, so in Kannada say ಲಾಡ್ಜ್ (laadj) or just ರೂಮ್ (room) for a place to stay; big hotels are called ಹೋಟೆಲ್ too when the context is clear. Ask: ಇಲ್ಲಿ ರೂಮ್ ಸಿಗುತ್ತಾ? (illi room siguttaa?, can I get a room here?), ರೂಮ್ ಖಾಲಿ ಇದೆಯಾ? (room khaali ideyaa?, is a room free?), ಒಂದು ರಾತ್ರಿಗೆ ಎಷ್ಟು? (ondu raatrige eshtu?, how much for one night?). "I need a room for two days" is ನನಗೆ ಎರಡು ದಿನಕ್ಕೆ ರೂಮ್ ಬೇಕು (nanage eradu dinakke room beku). ಸಿಗು (sigu) means "to be available / be found".

## Luggage — ಸಾಮಾನು (saamaanu)

The Kannada word for luggage, belongings or things is ಸಾಮಾನು (saamaanu); ಲಗೇಜ್ (lagej) is also used. Useful phrases: ನನ್ನ ಸಾಮಾನು ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇಡಲಿ? (nanna saamaanu elli idali?, where shall I keep my luggage?), ಇದನ್ನು ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಹಿಡಿದುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ (idannu svalpa hididukolli, please hold this for a moment), ನನ್ನ ಬ್ಯಾಗ್ ಕಾಣ್ತಿಲ್ಲ (nanna byaag kaantilla, I can't find my bag). On buses, keep your bag near you; to make room, people say ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಜಾಗ ಕೊಡಿ (svalpa jaaga kodi, please give a little space).

Also asked as: luggage in Kannada, where can I keep my bag, I lost my bag, belongings, saamaanu meaning

## I have reached — ತಲುಪಿದೆ (talupide)

<!-- topic: everyday-conversation -->

"I have reached" in Kannada is ನಾನು ತಲುಪಿದೆ (naanu talupide), from ತಲುಪು (talupu, to reach); in speech people also simply say ಬಂದೆ (bande, I've come). Families always ask you to call when you arrive: ಮನೆ ತಲುಪಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (mane talupida mele fon maadi, call after you reach home). To say you are on the way: ದಾರಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ (daariyalli iddeeni, I'm on the way) or ಬರ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ (bartaa iddeeni, I'm coming). "I'll be late" is ಲೇಟ್ ಆಗುತ್ತೆ (let aagutte).

Also asked as: how to say I have reached in Kannada, I arrived, I'm on the way, call when you reach, I'll be late

## Have a safe journey — ಹುಷಾರಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ ಬನ್ನಿ (hushaaraagi hogi banni)

<!-- topic: greetings -->

To wish someone a safe journey in Kannada, say ಹುಷಾರಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ ಬನ್ನಿ (hushaaraagi hogi banni), "go carefully and come back"; to a friend, ಹುಷಾರಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ ಬಾ (hushaaraagi hogi baa). The formal written wish is ಶುಭ ಪ್ರಯಾಣ (shubha prayaana, happy journey), seen on signs and in messages. Families add ತಲುಪಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ಫೋನ್ ಮಾಡಿ (talupida mele fon maadi, call when you reach). The traveller replies ಹೋಗಿ ಬರುತ್ತೇನೆ (hogi baruttene, I'll go and come back), spoken ಹೋಗಿ ಬರ್ತೀನಿ (hogi barteeni).

Also asked as: how to say have a safe trip in Kannada, happy journey, bon voyage, travel safely
