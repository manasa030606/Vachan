# Hindi conversation situations

## Introductions — आपसे मिलकर ख़ुशी हुई (aapse milkar khushee huee)

<!-- topic: introductions -->

To introduce yourself in Hindi: नमस्ते, मेरा नाम आशा है (namaste, meraa naam Asha hai — hello, my name is Asha). Ask the other person: आपका नाम क्या है? (aapkaa naam kyaa hai? — what is your name?) and आप कहाँ से हैं? (aap kahaan se hain? — where are you from?). Answer with मैं दिल्ली से हूँ (main Dillee se hoon — I am from Delhi). Finish with आपसे मिलकर ख़ुशी हुई (aapse milkar khushee huee — nice to meet you).

## At a restaurant — मुझे खाना चाहिए (mujhe khaanaa chaahiye)

<!-- topic: restaurant -->

In a Hindi-speaking restaurant the waiter may ask क्या लेंगे? (kyaa lenge? — what will you have?). Answer with मुझे खाना चाहिए (mujhe khaanaa chaahiye — I want food) or एक चाय दीजिए (ek chaay deejiye — one tea, please). Ask for water: पानी दीजिए (paani deejiye). Praise the food: बहुत अच्छा है (bahut achchhaa hai — it is very good). At the end ask बिल कितना हुआ? (bil kitnaa huaa? — how much is the bill?) and say धन्यवाद (dhanyavaad — thank you).

## Shopping — यह कितने का है? (yah kitne kaa hai?)

<!-- topic: shopping; level: elementary -->

At a Hindi-speaking shop or market, point and ask यह कितने का है? (yah kitne kaa hai? — how much is this?). The seller may answer सौ रुपये (sau rupaye — a hundred rupees). If it seems expensive, say बहुत महँगा है (bahut mahangaa hai — it's too expensive) and थोड़ा कम कीजिए (thodaa kam keejiye — please lower it a little). To say how many you want: मुझे दो चाहिए (mujhe do chaahiye — I want two). To agree: ठीक है, ले लूँगा (theek hai, le loongaa — okay, I'll take it); a woman says ले लूँगी (le loongee).

## Travel — एक टिकट दीजिए (ek tikat deejiye)

<!-- topic: travel; level: elementary -->

When travelling in Hindi, ask बस अड्डा कहाँ है? (bas addaa kahaan hai? — where is the bus station?). At the counter say एक टिकट दीजिए (ek tikat deejiye — one ticket, please). Ask the time: जयपुर की बस कब है? (Jaipur kee bas kab hai? — when is the bus to Jaipur?). Check the route: क्या यह ट्रेन आगरा जाती है? (kyaa yah tren Aagraa jaatee hai? — does this train go to Agra?). Ask the fare: टिकट कितने का है? (tikat kitne kaa hai?).

## Asking for directions — कहाँ है? (kahaan hai?)

<!-- topic: directions; level: elementary -->

To ask the way in Hindi, say the place and add कहाँ है? (kahaan hai? — where is it?): अस्पताल कहाँ है? (aspataal kahaan hai? — where is the hospital?). Direction words: बाएँ (baayen — left), दाएँ (daayen — right), सीधे जाइए (seedhe jaaiye — go straight). पास (paas — near), दूर (door — far), यहाँ (yahaan — here), वहाँ (vahaan — there). To say you did not follow: मुझे समझ नहीं आया (mujhe samajh nahin aayaa — I didn't understand).

## Everyday conversation — खाना खाया? (khaanaa khaayaa?)

<!-- topic: everyday-conversation -->

In everyday Hindi conversation, after आप कैसे हैं? (aap kaise hain? — how are you?) and मैं ठीक हूँ (main theek hoon — I am fine), friends often ask खाना खाया? (khaanaa khaayaa? — have you eaten?). Ask about family: घर पर सब ठीक है? (ghar par sab theek hai? — is everything fine at home?). Ask about the day: आज क्या किया? (aaj kyaa kiyaa? — what did you do today?). Say goodbye with फिर मिलेंगे (phir milenge — see you again).
