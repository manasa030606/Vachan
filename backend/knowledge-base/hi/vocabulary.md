# Everyday Hindi vocabulary

## Family words

Hindi family words: माँ (maa, mother), पिता (pitaa, father — at home often पापा, paapaa, or पिताजी, pitaajee), भाई (bhaai, brother), बहन (bahan, sister), बेटा (betaa, son), बेटी (betee, daughter), दादा (daadaa, father's father), दादी (daadee, father's mother), नाना (naanaa, mother's father), नानी (naanee, mother's mother). A friend is दोस्त (dost). Hindi has different words for grandparents on the father's and the mother's side. For older and younger siblings it adds बड़ा (badaa, big) or छोटा (chhotaa, small): बड़ा भाई (badaa bhaai, elder brother), छोटी बहन (chhotee bahan, younger sister).

## Food and drink

<!-- topic: food -->

Hindi food words: खाना (khaanaa, food; also the verb "to eat"), पानी (paani, water), दूध (doodh, milk), चाय (chaay, tea), रोटी (rotee, flatbread), चावल (chaaval, rice), दाल (daal, lentils), सब्ज़ी (sabzee, vegetables / vegetable dish), दही (dahee, curd / yoghurt), नमक (namak, salt), चीनी (cheenee, sugar), फल (phal, fruit). "I want food" is मुझे खाना चाहिए (mujhe khaanaa chaahiye), and "I want water" is मुझे पानी चाहिए (mujhe paani chaahiye).

## Numbers 1 to 10

<!-- topic: numbers -->

Hindi numbers from one to ten: 1 एक (ek), 2 दो (do), 3 तीन (teen), 4 चार (chaar), 5 पाँच (paanch), 6 छह (chhah), 7 सात (saat), 8 आठ (aath), 9 नौ (nau), 10 दस (das). Hindi also has its own digits, sometimes seen on signs and in books: १ २ ३ ४ ५ ६ ७ ८ ९ १०. Numbers come before the noun: दो किताबें (do kitaaben, two books), तीन दोस्त (teen dost, three friends).

## Colours

<!-- topic: colours; level: elementary -->

Hindi colour words: लाल (laal, red), हरा (haraa, green), नीला (neelaa, blue), पीला (peelaa, yellow), सफ़ेद (safed, white), काला (kaalaa, black). Colour words ending in -आ (-aa) change with the noun's gender and number: काला कुत्ता (kaalaa kuttaa, black dog), काली बिल्ली (kaalee billee, black cat), नीले कपड़े (neele kapde, blue clothes). Words like लाल and सफ़ेद never change: लाल गुलाब (laal gulaab, red rose).

## Time words

<!-- topic: time; level: elementary -->

Useful Hindi time words: आज (aaj, today), कल (kal, tomorrow and also yesterday — the verb tense tells you which), अब (ab, now), अभी (abhee, right now), बाद में (baad mein, later), सुबह (subah, morning), दोपहर (dopahar, afternoon), शाम (shaam, evening), रात (raat, night). Example: कल सुबह आइए (kal subah aaiye, "please come tomorrow morning"). With a past verb, कल means yesterday: मैं कल आया (main kal aayaa, "I came yesterday").

## Days of the week

<!-- topic: time; level: elementary -->

The Hindi days of the week all end in वार (vaar, day): सोमवार (somvaar, Monday), मंगलवार (mangalvaar, Tuesday), बुधवार (budhvaar, Wednesday), गुरुवार (guruvaar, Thursday), शुक्रवार (shukravaar, Friday), शनिवार (shanivaar, Saturday), रविवार (ravivaar, Sunday). To say "on Monday", add को (ko): सोमवार को (somvaar ko). To ask "what day is it today?": आज कौन-सा दिन है? (aaj kaun-saa din hai?).
